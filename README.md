# Kit Checker

**Kit Checker** is a fully offline, privacy-first app that helps prehospital
and emergency care practitioners keep their equipment check process as
rigorous as their clinical practice.

## What it does

Kit Checker lets you build a digital hierarchy that mirrors your physical kit
bag — down to individual compartments and items — exactly as it exists in
real life. Once your structure is set up, you can conduct full kit checks,
record what was checked and when, and build a reliable evidence trail over
time, all without an internet connection.

## Why it's different

Most kit-checking tools fall into one of two camps: overly simple, fixed-
structure checklists that don't reflect how real kit bags are organised, or
heavyweight, cloud-dependent enterprise platforms built for whole
organisations rather than individual practitioners or small teams.

Kit Checker sits in the gap between the two:

- **Structural fidelity** — your digital kit hierarchy matches your physical
  kit, not a generic template.
- **Fully offline** — no internet connection required, ever, to use the app.
- **No data collection** — no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, nothing
  leaves your device without your explicit action. This is a non-negotiable
  design principle, not a feature toggle.
- **User autonomy** — the app ships empty. You define your own kit
  structure, categories, and schedules, rather than working around someone
  else's assumptions about how your kit is organised.

## Who it's for

Kit Checker is built for anyone who is professionally or operationally
accountable for the readiness of a kit bag, including:

- HEMS and air ambulance crew
- Community First Responders (CFR)
- Event medical teams
- Search and rescue (SAR) and wilderness medicine practitioners
- Offshore and industrial medics
- NHS HART teams, ski patrol, and St John Ambulance volunteers

## Privacy

Kit Checker collects no personal data, usage data, or analytics of any kind.
Everything you enter — your kit hierarchy, check records, and schedules —
stays on your device unless you explicitly choose to export or share it.
See our full [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md) for details.
