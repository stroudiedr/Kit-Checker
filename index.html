The gap

Between too simple and too heavy

Most kit-checking tools sit at one extreme or the other. Kit Checker was built for the space in between.

Fixed checklists

Basic apps assume every kit bag looks the same. They give you a flat list, not a structure — no compartments, no hierarchy, no way to reflect how your bag actually works.

Kit Checker

Your hierarchy, your items, your schedules — checked and recorded entirely offline, on the device already in your pocket.

Enterprise platforms

Agency-wide systems are cloud-dependent and built for whole organisations. They're overkill for an individual practitioner or a small team who just needs their own kit checked properly.

Design principles

Built the way kit itself is trusted

Four commitments that shaped every decision in the app, from the data model up.

01 STRUCTURE

Structural fidelity

Your digital kit hierarchy matches your physical kit — not a generic template someone else designed.

02 CONNECTIVITY

Fully offline

No internet connection is ever required to build, check, or record against your kit.

03 DATA

No data collection

No accounts, no analytics, no telemetry. Nothing leaves your device without your explicit action.

04 CONTROL

User autonomy

The app ships empty. You define your own structure, categories, and schedules from the ground up.

Built for

Anyone accountable for a kit bag

Kit Checker is designed around individual practitioners and small teams — not agency-wide procurement.

Privacy

Nothing to collect, because nothing leaves the device

Kit Checker was built offline-first from day one. That's not a setting you can find and turn off — it's the foundation the app is built on.

  • No accounts. There's nothing to sign up for and nothing to log into.
  • No analytics. Your usage of the app is never measured, tracked, or reported.
  • No telemetry. The app doesn't phone home, ever.
  • No sharing without action. Data only ever leaves your device if you explicitly export or share it yourself.
